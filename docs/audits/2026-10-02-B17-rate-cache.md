## 2. What it does

```
PMS sync (every 15 min)          lib/mews-sync.ts → reservations stored, run logged
  └─ rate step (after the log)   lib/rate-sync.ts, time-boxed, never fails the sync
       ├─ room charges           every run    MEWS order items · Apaleo time slices
       ├─ lowest public price    ~hourly      MEWS rates/pricing/restrictions/availability · Apaleo offers
       └─ day snapshot           first run after local midnight
                ↓
  rate_nights (14 rows/hotel)  rate_snapshots (14 rows/hotel/day, 400 days)
                ↓
  lib/rate-outlook.ts → brief (rate_alert) · Ask (hotel data + "Rates" chip) · Home (strip)
```

- **Average rate (ADR)** = room revenue ÷ paid rooms, per hotel-local night. A room counts only if its charges for that night sum above zero, so a comp room or a stay whose charges aren't posted yet doesn't drag the average down. A night with no paid room shows a dash, never zero.
- **Selling from** = the cheapest price a guest booking **now** could get **arriving that night**: a public, active rate (derived ones count), open for that guest, in a room type with a room left. A night where everything is sold out or closed is "nothing on sale"; a night the PMS couldn't answer for is left blank. A price not confirmed in the last 6 hours isn't shown.
- **Pickup** = today's snapshot minus yesterday's, per night, net of cancellations. It is reported only when the two snapshots were taken about a day apart (±4 h). The total covers tonight and the next 12 nights — the nights both snapshots share — and the strip says "next 2 weeks".
- **Where it shows.** Home: each night's average rate under its column, plus one line ("Selling from €95 tonight · Booked yesterday, next 2 weeks: +5 room nights"). No accent is spent. Brief: the `rate_alert` paragraph gets the revenue signal — facts only, no recommended price, no last-year comparison. Ask: per-night rates in the hotel data, and a "Rates" source chip.
- **The Sheet import** has no charges and no prices. Home says "Your booking sheet doesn't include rates", and pickup still works.

A separate agent read the whole diff against the MEWS and Apaleo docs. What it found, and what was done:

| 2 | A MEWS restriction with exceptions was treated as never closing. | 3 | "Couldn't tell" was stored as "nothing on sale" (no service, no public rate, missing availability, Apaleo 422/403, wrong channel) | Medium | Three outcomes per night: price, nothing on sale, unknown. | 4 | Every MEWS "Bookable" service was treated as rooms (parking at €15) | Medium | Only the service most of the fortnight's stays belong to is priced. With no stays, the only nightly service; if there are several, "unknown" |
| 5 | The rate step could hold up or break the sync (no time budget, before the log; failed checks retried every 15 min) | Medium | It runs after the run is logged and stamped. ## 6. Built from the docs, not yet seen live — what to suspect first

This session could reach neither PMS (the MEWS demo and Apaleo are blocked from its network). Everything below follows the published docs and is pinned by fixtures, not by a real payload. If §4 item 4 disagrees, look here, in this order:

1. **MEWS future-night charges.** Assumed: a reservation's future nights already exist as `SpaceOrder` order items with `AccountingState` `Open`. If future nights are `Inactive` until posted, the average rate will be blank for future nights. The fix is to include them, accepting zero-value ones as "not paid".
2. **MEWS availability formula.** `UsableResources − Occupied − OtherServiceReservationCount − HouseUse + PublicAvailabilityAdjustment`. If "Selling from" quotes a room type the grid shows as full, this is why.
3. **MEWS restriction intervals.** `EndUtc` is read as inclusive (the last time unit's start).
4. **Apaleo `baseAmount`.** Assumed: the room price without included services. If a rate includes breakfast, the average rate excludes it — the room-only figure — which is intended.
5. **Apaleo offers channel.** `Direct`, two adults. A property that sells nothing on Direct gets "unknown" rather than a wrong price.

## 8. Not done, on purpose

- **The Sheet import's rates.** A price column could feed the average rate, but exports don't say whether their amounts include VAT, and the agreed basis is net. Decide the basis (or a per-hotel VAT setting) when a Sheet pilot asks.
- **"ADR & RevPAR" and "Pickup & pace" stay locked tiles.** They are Revenue Management's surface (month to date, RevPAR, pace curves). B17 is the cache and the sentence in the brief.
- **Last-year comparisons.** No history yet. Snapshots are kept 400 days so this becomes possible.
- **Filtering MEWS order items by date** (finding 12). The `ConsumedUtc` filter's behaviour for future nights isn't confirmed. Revisit if MEWS rate limits ever bite.
- **Pricing recommendations of any kind.** The brief states facts. `AI_ACT_PROMPTS.md` §R: revenue management is not Annex III, and nothing is priced on personal traits.

## 9. Found along the way (not B17's to fix)

- **MEWS occupancy counts every service's reservations.** `reservations/getAll` isn't filtered by service. A MEWS hotel that sells parking or a meeting room as a reservable service would see those counted as rooms on the strip, in the brief and in the rate cache alike. Check on the first MEWS pilot. Logged in `ROADMAP.md` §3.2.
- **The marketing sample brief claims a last-year comparison** ("last summer that Thursday closed at 90%", `app/[lang]/sample-brief/content.ts`) that the product cannot make yet. Logged in §3.4.
